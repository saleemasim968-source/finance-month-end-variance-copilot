The challenge

Month-end is the most time-critical week in finance. It is also where the most manual work hides.

01

Slow close

Every site exports its own report. Controllers re-key accounts, convert thousands and currencies, and tie out totals by hand before analysis can even start.

02

Sites that can't be compared

Different charts of accounts, cost-centre structures and units mean "cost per wafer" means something different at every fab.

03

Commentary nobody can trace

Narratives mix "we produced less" with "we spent more", and the figures in the text can't be traced back to a source line.

How it works

Four steps, one command, full lineage.

  1. 1

    Harmonise

    Raw files load unchanged. SQL converts periods, units and currencies, and maintained mapping tables put every row on the group chart of accounts while keeping the local codes beside it.

  2. 2

    Check

    Duplicates, missing actuals and unmapped codes are reported with file and line. Every site and month is reconciled to source; an unmapped code stops the run.

  3. 3

    Analyse

    A flexed budget splits each variance into a volume effect and a spend effect. Fixed, configurable thresholds flag only what is material.

  4. 4

    Explain

    Commentary is drafted from the calculated figures only. Any number not in the data is rejected, cost-centre owners add the cause, and a controller approves.

AI you can audit

The model writes words. The pipeline owns the numbers.

Generative AI speeds up commentary, but finance can't accept a figure nobody can trace. Every control below runs on every draft, whether a language model or the built-in template wrote it.

  • Number check. Every figure in a draft must match the calculated data, or the draft is rejected and rewritten.
  • Human sign-off. Nothing is released without a controller's approval, recorded with role and time.
  • Data minimisation. Only rounded, aggregated figures for one item are sent to the model. No person-level data exists anywhere.
  • Full lineage. Every reported figure points to its source file and line.
  • Works offline. Without an AI key, a template drafts the commentary and every other control still applies.
